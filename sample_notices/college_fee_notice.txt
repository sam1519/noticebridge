SUNRISE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY - EXAMINATION CELL

Subject: Semester Exam Fee and Hall Ticket Collection

All students of the 5th semester are informed that the examination fee of Rs. 1,800 must be paid before 5 December 2026. Students who fail to pay the fee will not be allowed to appear for the semester examination.

Hall tickets will be issued from 10 December 2026 at the examination cell. Students should bring their college ID card and the fee receipt. The examinations will begin on 15 December 2026.
